Chinese that runs continuously inside school from primary to high school, with every stage aligned to exam levels and progress parents can read, is not on offer anywhere yet.

03 · 对标03 · Alignment一条主轴，连接所有中文考试One spine, linked to every Chinese exam

课程不包含考试，也不为考试而教。我们用 ACTFL 做主轴，因为它描述的是「能用中文做什么」；再把每个 ACTFL 等级对应到各个考试的水平。这样同一套课程设计可以同时对齐 YCT、HSK、TOCFL 和 AP，家庭需要哪一种证书，就知道孩子什么时候准备好了。TNIS 是 AP 学校，AP Chinese 本身也是按 ACTFL 设计的。Exams are not part of the course, and we do not teach to them. ACTFL is the spine because it describes what a student can do with Chinese; each ACTFL level is then matched to the level of each exam. One course design therefore aligns with YCT, HSK, TOCFL and AP at once, and families can see when their child is ready for whichever certificate they need. TNIS is an AP school, and AP Chinese is itself designed around ACTFL.

台湾的大学（TOCFL）Universities in Taiwan (TOCFL)TOCFL 有官方 CEFR 对应：Level 2 = A2，Level 3 = B1。不少大学的中文授课学系以 A2 为门槛（例：国立联合大学「应达 A2（含）级以上」）；热门学系和奖学金通常看 B1。TOCFL maps officially to CEFR: Level 2 = A2, Level 3 = B1. Many Chinese-taught degree programmes set A2 as the minimum (e.g. National United University: "A2 or above"); competitive programmes and scholarships usually look for B1.
香港大学（HKU）The University of Hong KongHKU 对持国际学历的申请人一般不要求中文（招生 FAQ：「Chinese is not a must for the admissions」），但部分专业会期望懂中文或粤语。对 TNIS 学生，中文是申请时的加分项，不是门槛。HKU generally does not require Chinese from applicants with international qualifications (admissions FAQ: "Chinese is not a must for the admissions"), though some programmes expect Chinese or Cantonese. For TNIS students, Chinese strengthens an application rather than gating it.
AP ChineseAP ChineseAP 课程的设计区间是 Intermediate 到 Pre-Advanced（参考 HKIS 的 AP Chinese：入学 Intermediate High，结业目标 Pre-Advanced）。G3 起步的学生到 G12 正好落在这个区间的上半段。AP courses are designed for the Intermediate to Pre-Advanced range (e.g. HKIS's AP Chinese: entry Intermediate High, end-of-course target Pre-Advanced). Students who start at G3 reach the upper half of that range by G12.
