From G3 to G12: the exam level each year reaches

Projected from Mandarin I's real pace this year: ten years from G3 reach about HSK 3 and AP 3 when language ability leads, and HSK 4 and AP 5 when exam alignment leads. A third period a week in G6–8 adds 90 periods and lifts the language-first G12 result from AP 3 to AP 4.

CrosswalkGrade, learning load and exam level

Language ability first

Today's classroom pace: about 1.8 new words a period in high school, with handwriting, sentence patterns and speaking practised alongside. About 80% of words are on the exam lists.

Exam alignment first

A faster new-word pace (about 2.7 a period in high school, or 13–14 a week), with words chosen from the exam lists, so about 95% count towards the exams.

"Within reach" means 80% of the next level's vocabulary, reachable with exam preparation. Grammar points are estimated from each level's grammar load in China's national Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards (GF 0025—2021), matched to the vocabulary reached. YCT is the test for young learners; HSK 3.0 levels follow the 2025 syllabus used from 13 Dec 2026; TOCFL is Taiwan's test. ACTFL and AP are approximate matches by vocabulary: speaking and task performance decide in the end.

Timetable optionWhat a third period in G6–8 buys

Moving G6–8 from 2 to 3 periods a week adds 30 periods a year, 90 in all. Everything else stays the same.
